Basecamp

Plan the trip, pack the pack, log the summit — all in one place. Basecamp ties your objective, your gear loadout, and your completion record into a single connected trip, instead of scattering them across notes apps, spreadsheets, and guidebooks.

Browse objectives

What it does

  • Search objectives (peaks, routes, trails) by region, difficulty, and season.
  • Build reusable gear loadouts with automatic weight totals.
  • Plan trips around an objective and track them from planned to completed.
  • Keep a Summit Log of everything you’ve finished.

Who it’s for

Backcountry hikers, backpackers, and mountaineers — especially college outdoor-club members, mountaineering-course students, and informal groups who plan trips together and want their planning and record-keeping to live in the same tool.
